For older devices the provider's gateway also accepts aes128-sha1-modp1024
in both phases. Don't use it: SHA-1 and DH group 2 (1,024 bits) are no
longer considered safe.

Who starts the tunnel: your gateway. The provider's gateway answers and
never initiates. During maintenance it restarts; your gateway must bring
the tunnel back by itself.
