<Intro>

These six labs are probes, not a lesson. They try the network-lab base, the image every Module 3 lab will start from, so that CI shows what works on its machines before any Module 3 topic is written. Four are Module 3 labs already, built as their topics' plans describe, which will move into those topics: a NAT gateway made step by step (NAT's worked example), big downloads that hang across a GRE tunnel (Layers and packets' debug), an office whose IPv6 half works (IPv6's debug), and BBR against CUBIC on a long, lossy link, measured three times. The other two build a route-based IPsec tunnel (a VTI) with BGP over it, and run a small internet of name servers, a load balancer, web servers, a NAT router and a slow link.

</Intro>
