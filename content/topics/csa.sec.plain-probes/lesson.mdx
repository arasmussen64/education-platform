<Intro>

These two labs are probes, not a lesson. They try the two plain lab bases Module 4 adds, so that CI shows what works on its machines before any Module 4 topic is written. One is on the policy base: a shop's refund rules written in Cedar and checked against requests made up at check time, with the base's three helpers (`ask`, `access-review` and `ca-whatif`) run on scenery. The other is on the threat-model base: a small threat model written as plain lines of text and checked by `tm-check` against a design.

</Intro>

<Recap>

When CI has run them, read the Labs job's log for each probe's timings and checks, then remove this topic: the authentication-and-authorization and threat-modeling topics will have labs of their own on these bases.

</Recap>
