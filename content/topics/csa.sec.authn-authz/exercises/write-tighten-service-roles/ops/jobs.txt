What each service account is for (written by the team that owns it)

reports   role: report-writer
  Reads orders and writes the report files. Once a quarter it exports a
  summary for finance. It changes no orders and deletes nothing.

deployer  role: release-deployer
  Opens a change for each release, then creates the release and updates
  it. A change is approved by a release approver, a person. The deployer
  never approves a change, its own least of all, and deletes nothing.

backup    role: backup-operator
  Reads the orders and every store's data, and writes them to the backup
  vault. It reads nothing back from the vault and deletes nothing: old
  backups are removed by the vault's own retention setting.

People with the release-approver role approve changes. Nobody, in any
role, approves a change they made themselves.
