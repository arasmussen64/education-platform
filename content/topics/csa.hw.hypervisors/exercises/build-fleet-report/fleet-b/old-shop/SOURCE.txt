What an EC2 T2 instance, the one general-purpose family AWS still lists
with the Xen hypervisor, presents to Linux: Xen's paravirtual network and
disk devices. In /sys their drivers are called vif and vbd (the kernel
modules are xen_netfront and xen_blkfront), and the disks are named xvda
and on. Sources: AWS, "General purpose instances",
https://docs.aws.amazon.com/ec2/latest/instancetypes/gp.html (read
2026-11-11); the Linux kernel source, drivers/net/xen-netfront.c,
drivers/block/xen-blkfront.c and drivers/xen/xenbus/xenbus_probe.c (read
2026-11-11); systemd-detect-virt(1).
