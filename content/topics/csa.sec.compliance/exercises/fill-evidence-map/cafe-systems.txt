The shop's new café: six systems, as they are today.

kiosk1   The self-service kiosk. A customer taps or inserts a card, and the
         kiosk reads the card number and sends it to the bank.
         Subnet 10.50.2.0/24.

cam1     The café's camera recorder. It never handles a card. It is on
         subnet 10.50.2.0/24 as well, and nothing filters traffic between
         machines on that subnet.

backup1  The backup server. Every night it copies kiosk1's database, which
         holds the day's card numbers, and keeps each copy for a month.
         Subnet 10.50.3.0/24. A firewall lets kiosk1, and nothing else,
         send to it.

menu1    The café's ordering site. Its pay page shows the bank's card form
         inside a frame: the card is typed into the bank's form and goes
         straight to the bank, never to menu1. menu1 serves the page that
         the frame sits in. Subnet 10.50.4.0/24.

mgmt1    The laptop the café's technician administers kiosk1 from, by
         remote desktop. Subnet 10.50.5.0/24. A firewall lets mgmt1 open
         remote-desktop connections to kiosk1.

music1   The café's music player, on the guest Wi-Fi, 10.50.90.0/24. A
         firewall drops everything between the guest Wi-Fi and every other
         subnet, in both directions. It holds no card data and nobody
         administers anything from it.
