Five of the shop's systems, as they are today.

pay1    The payments server. The tills send it card numbers, and it sends
        them on to the bank. Subnet 10.30.1.0/24.

print1  A print server for receipts and labels. No card number is ever
        sent to it. Subnet 10.30.1.0/24, the same as pay1, and nothing
        filters traffic between machines on that subnet.

jump1   The admins' jump host. Admins sign in to it, and from it to pay1
        over SSH. Subnet 10.30.9.0/24. A firewall lets jump1, and nothing
        else there, open SSH connections to pay1.

web1    The shop's website. It shows the basket, then sends the shopper's
        browser to the bank's own payment page, where the card is typed.
        No card number reaches web1. Subnet 10.30.5.0/24.

wiki1   The staff wiki, on the office network, 10.40.0.0/16. A firewall
        drops everything between the office network and the three subnets
        above, in both directions. Nobody administers a card system from
        the wiki, and it holds no card data.
