<Intro>

This lab is a probe, not a lesson. It tries the payments-server base, the image that the server labs of Audit logging, Vulnerability management and Compliance basics will start from, so that CI shows what works on its machines before any of those topics is written. One lab uses the three mechanisms most likely to behave differently there: a journal sealed in a journal namespace (with a check that tampers with a copy and must notice), OpenSCAP run against the pinned benchmark, and OSV-Scanner run offline against its seven frozen records.

</Intro>
